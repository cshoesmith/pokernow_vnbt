vNBT Poker - Chrome extension for Poker Now
===========================================

What it does (on pokernow.com game pages):
  - Beer-themed table with a title (default "vNBT Poker Challenge")
  - Chip stack chart for every player, in the bottom-left corner, with a
    gold coin where someone bought back in, and a Winnings view (stack minus
    what they put in); its Table view shows each player's ledger result
  - Live "win chance" badge next to your seat during a hand
  - Untappd: if your Poker Now name is your Untappd username, your seat
    shows your latest check-in (beer, brewery, rating, ABV
    and photo)
  - Hides the promo link, chat and video buttons (each can be turned back on)

Install (about 1 minute):
  1. Unzip this file somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. Documents\vNBT Poker).
     Don't delete the folder afterwards - Chrome runs the extension from it.
  2. In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder
     (the one that contains manifest.json).
  5. Reload your Poker Now tab.

Tip - show your beer:
  Join the game with your Untappd username as your Poker Now name, and
  your seat will show what you're drinking. Use the username from your
  profile link (untappd.com/user/<username>), not your display name.
  Your Untappd profile needs to be public; new check-ins show up within
  a few minutes.

Settings:
  Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin "vNBT Poker",
  then click its icon to turn features on or off and change the table title.

Updating to a new version:
  Replace the folder's contents with the new files, then click the
  reload arrow on vNBT Poker in chrome://extensions.

Note: Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" message when it
starts. That's normal for extensions installed this way - click the X to keep it.
