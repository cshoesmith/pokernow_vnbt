vNBT Poker - Chrome extension for Poker Now
===========================================

What it does (on pokernow.com game pages):
  - Beer-themed table with a title (default "vNBT Poker Challenge")
  - Table video: live TV on the felt, under the cards. Under "Table video"
    in the popup, pick a list (China, World news, or one of 30 countries
    under Countries) and a channel; search, or step through with the arrows.
    Many channels are off air or only shown in their own country: one that
    won't play is marked dead and hidden from the menu, and "Check channels"
    tests the whole list and hides every dead one (it carries on if the
    popup closes). "Show dead channels" brings them back; marks last 3 days.
    Channels already known to be dead come hidden to start with. Muted
    unless "Sound" is ticked (it then starts once you click in the game). Pick "Nothing"
    to go back to the felt. The small button at the top of the felt pops
    the video out into its own window (the table goes back to its felt
    until you close it)
  - Chip stack chart for every player, in the bottom-left corner, with a
    gold coin where someone bought back in, and a Winnings view (stack minus
    what they put in); its Table view shows each player's ledger result
  - Live "win chance" badge next to your seat during a hand
  - Saves a screenshot to Downloads\vNBT Poker when a pot goes to a royal
    flush, straight flush, four of a kind or ace-high straight (the game
    tab must be in front). Chrome lists this as access to all sites; only
    the game tab is ever captured
  - Untappd: if your Poker Now name is your Untappd username, a scrolling
    banner above the table title shows your avatar and latest check-in
    (beer, brewery, rating, ABV and photo). If someone's names differ,
    pair them up in the popup's name list (only on your own screen)
  - Win streak flames: a player who wins hands in a row gets flames round
    their name - small after one win, bigger after two, biggest from three.
    Any hand they're dealt into and don't win (folding too) puts them out.
    Five in a row sets off a nuclear blast over their name, and so does
    every win after that until the streak ends
  - Chip leader crown: a gold crown on the name of whoever has the most
    chips, moving as each pot is awarded (nobody wears it on a tie)
  - Card backs: five silhouette pin-up dancers on the backs of the cards,
    dealt at random and reshuffled every hand. Pick one for the whole
    table, or Poker Now's own, under "Card backs" in the popup
  - Turn timer beeps: a beer can opening when your turn
    starts, then a double beep at 10 seconds left,
    then a beep every second from 5 (higher for the last 3). Works with
    the game tab in the background, once you've clicked in the game.
    Set the volume and try it with the slider and Test button in the popup
  - Hides the promo link, chat and video buttons (each can be turned back on)

Install (about 1 minute):
  1. Unzip this file somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. Documents\vNBT Poker).
     Don't delete the folder afterwards - Chrome runs the extension from it.
  2. In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder
     (the one that contains manifest.json).
  5. Reload your Poker Now tab.

Tip - show your beer:
  Join the game with your Untappd username as your Poker Now name, and
  the banner will show what you're drinking. Use the username from your
  profile link (untappd.com/user/<username>), not your display name.
  Your Untappd profile needs to be public; new check-ins show up within
  a few minutes.

Settings:
  Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin "vNBT Poker",
  then click its icon to turn features on or off and change the table title.
  If the beer table goes missing while Poker Now has a holiday theme on
  (Halloween, Christmas...), tick "Holidays workaround".

Updating to a new version:
  Replace the folder's contents with the new files, then click the
  reload arrow on vNBT Poker in chrome://extensions.

Note: Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" message when it
starts. That's normal for extensions installed this way - click the X to keep it.
